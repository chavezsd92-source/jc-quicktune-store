FAQ

Common questions

What do I get after purchase?

A calibrated full BIN matched to your software family notes, flash checklist, and recommended adapt clear. Optional log review if purchased.

Will Comfort still make power?

Yes. Comfort mainly softens burble and part-load boost behavior. Dig into the pedal and the Stage torque path is still there — Sport is optimized for the fun personality.

What about the 1F053C limp code?

The base stack includes the maximum-acceleration safety calibration and ACCEL limiter handling that commonly trips after large torque unlocks on MG1.

Do I need a dyno?

Not required. The product was developed with street Quickflash + datalog validation. A dyno is welcome; log review add-on helps either way.

Can I go back to stock?

Yes if you save your original read before flashing. We recommend keeping stock and pre-tune backups offline.
