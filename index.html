What you’re actually getting

Comfort / daily

Burble activation is not “on for everything.” The base overrun path is calmed down so you’re not lighting money on fire every time you lift for a red light. Part-load boost targets are pulled back so cruise isn’t running Stage boost for no reason. Closed-loop cruise still sits around stoich like it should.

You can still make power if you bury the pedal — Comfort isn’t a governor. It just stops acting like a show car in school zones.

Sport / Sport+

This is where the burble duration and ignition crackle live. Pedal maps are snappy but smoothed so tip-in isn’t a light-switch. Throttle aggression is firm, not violent. Torque / load ceilings are raised the way commercial files do — including the annoying stock caps that leave you stuck around ~399 Nm while the “power tables” look higher on paper.

Also includes the 1F053C max-acceleration safety work. We hit drivetrain limp twice after the big torque unlock before that was sorted. You probably don’t want that surprise.

Flash requirements (non-negotiable)

  1. Unlock the DME first. Use B48 Quickflash (or equivalent full-flash unlock path). After unlock, Quickflash should show unlock detected / full flash capable — not locked stock boot behavior.
  2. Read and save stock. Keep the original BIN offline. If something goes sideways, that’s your way home.
  3. Full flash this tune BIN. Partial writes / wrong software family / mismatched SWFL are how people brick evenings. We match to your platform notes (e.g. 080.017.x style family on our development car) — tell us your exact SWFL/SWFK from Quickflash before we send a file.
  4. Stable power. Battery tender recommended. Don’t flash on a dying battery in a parking lot.
  5. After flash: clear DTCs, clear DME adaptations once, easy drive before you go hunting WOT records.

If you only have a locked DME and no unlock tool, stop here. This product assumes you (or your shop) already solved unlock + full flash. We’re selling the calibration, not the unlock service.
