Flash requirements

  1. DME unlock first. Use B48 Quickflash (or equivalent). The DME must already be unlocked for full flash. This BIN does not unlock a locked DME.
  2. Read and save stock. Keep the original BIN offline.
  3. Tell us stock vs modified. Base Stage 1 numbers assume all-stock hardware. List every bolt-on or fuel change up front.
  4. Full flash this tune BIN. Partial writes are not supported. Send your SWFL/SWFK from Quickflash before we deliver a file.
  5. Stable power + aftercare. Battery tender recommended. After flash: clear DTCs, clear DME adaptations once, easy miles first.

If the DME is still locked, stop here. We’re selling the Stage 1 calibration — not a DME unlock service.
